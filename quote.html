Package math
(blade qty x blade cost + equipment cost) x markup = package total.
Package total ÷ blade qty = price per blade. Equipment is FREE.
Type a $ on an equipment line (like "the $2,000 saw for $500") and that $ comes off what the blades carry.
2+ blade types: each blade gets its share by cost, so every line has its own per-blade price.
